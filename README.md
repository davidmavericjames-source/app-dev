**The Punisher** (real name: *Frank Castle*) is an antihero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, known for his relentless war on crime using lethal methods after the tragic loss of his family.

## Character Overview

A former U.S. Marine Scout Sniper and Force Recon officer, Frank Castle became a vigilante after his wife and children were killed in crossfire at Central Park. His combat experience and tactical discipline are central to his operations, often tracked under designations like `Target: CASTLE_FRANK`.

### Key Live-Action Appearances
1. The Punisher (2004 Film)
2. Daredevil Season 2 (2016 Series)
3. The Punisher Seasons 1–2 (2017–2019 Series)

### Signature Gear & Supporting Characters

- Micro (David Lieberman)
